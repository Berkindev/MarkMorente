## Labelling basics

Both countries require fibre content and dealer identification on clothing, and the rules differ. We sew in the labels you specify, so confirm the final text with your compliance team or customs broker.

### United States

The FTC requires textile clothing covered by the Textile and Wool Acts to show fibre content, the country of origin and the identity of the manufacturer or dealer, which can be a company name or an RN number. A separate FTC rule, the Care Labeling Rule, requires care instructions to be permanently attached to clothing. Wool suits fall under the Wool Products Labeling Act as well.

### Canada

Under Canada's Textile Labelling Act, as explained by the Competition Bureau, labels must show fibre content, with generic fibre names and percentages in both English and French, plus dealer identity. Dealer identity can be a name and postal address or a CA identification number. Products sold in Quebec can have additional language requirements.

For private label orders we add your brand label, size label, and fibre and care labels during production. Send us the artwork and text and we confirm before cutting.

## Shipping to the US and Canada

Air freight is the usual choice for stock lots, samples and fast reorders. Sea freight in containers suits larger orders to East Coast, West Coast or Canadian ports. We deliver door-to-door or hand over to your forwarder, and quote shipping per destination.

Import duties on suits into the US and Canada depend on fabric, construction and the tariff classification, and US tariff rules have changed often in recent years. Work with a licensed customs broker to confirm the HTS or Canadian tariff code and the duty before you place the order.
