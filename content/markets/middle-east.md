## Why Middle East buyers source suits from Istanbul

Middle East buyers source suits from Istanbul because it is close, production is quick, and Turkish factories make the ceremony styles their customers ask for. Istanbul is a few hours by air from the Gulf and the Levant, and Iraq can be reached by truck.

Mark Morente has produced men's suits for more than 20 years. Our factory has capacity of 1000+ suits per day and typically finish production in 12-15 days, depending on quantity, model and fabric availability.

We work with businesses only: importers, re-exporters, wholesalers, menswear retailers, wedding and formalwear shops, and corporate buyers. The smallest order is one lot.

## Wedding and ceremony tuxedos

Weddings and formal events are a large part of the suit trade in the region. Retailers stock grooms' tuxedos, suits for brothers and close relatives, and smart 2-piece suits for guests. Our [tuxedos](/mens-suit-manufacturing/tuxedos) and [wedding suits](/mens-suit-manufacturing/wedding-suits) are the ranges Middle East buyers ask about most.

Styles that sell well:

- White, ivory and cream dinner jackets for summer and evening weddings
- Black and midnight navy tuxedos with shawl or peak lapels
- Jacquard, velvet-look, sequin and rhinestone jackets for the groom
- Slim and modern fit 2-piece suits in navy, grey, blue and beige
- 3-piece suits with waistcoat for relatives and guests

## Lighter fabrics for a hot climate

Most Gulf buyers order light and breathable cloth. Lighter wool blends and fabrics with a softer, less structured build sell best in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Qatar, where many events take place in air-conditioned halls but guests still travel in summer heat. [Linen summer suits](/mens-suit-manufacturing) also sell for daytime events. In Iraq, Jordan and Lebanon winters are cooler, so buyers often split orders between summer and mid-weight fabrics. See our [fabrics](/fabrics) page for options.

### UAE and Dubai re-export

Dubai is the main trading hub for many buyers in the region. Wholesalers there buy suits for local shops and for re-export to other Gulf states, East Africa and South Asia. Re-exporters usually ask for broad size runs, neutral labelling or their own label, and packing that keeps suits in good condition through a second shipment. If you re-export from a free zone, tell us your packing and labelling needs before production.

### Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Qatar

Retailers in Riyadh, Jeddah, Kuwait City and Doha look for clean finishing and premium details. Saudi Arabia and other Gulf states have their own conformity and labelling rules for imported goods, so check with your clearing agent what applies to apparel before the first order.

### Iraq and Erbil

Iraq can be reached by road from Turkey. Trucks cross from Turkey at the Habur border gate and reach Erbil, Duhok and other cities in the Kurdistan Region, with onward routes to Baghdad and the south. Erbil buyers often order wedding suits and tuxedos ahead of the busy season, and air cargo to Erbil or Baghdad covers urgent orders.

### Jordan and Lebanon

Buyers in Amman and Beirut order modern business suits and wedding styles. Sea freight to Aqaba or Beirut suits larger orders, and air covers stock lots.

Each order is quoted individually, with price and lead time per model and quantity. We ship door-to-door or to your forwarder, and quote shipping per destination.
