## Duties and paperwork: EU, UK and the rest of Europe

For EU buyers, the EU-Türkiye Customs Union means industrial goods such as suits move with zero customs duties when accompanied by an A.TR movement certificate. For UK buyers, the UK-Turkey trade agreement gives preferential tariffs if the goods meet its rules of origin. Your customs broker should confirm the details for your product code before the first shipment.

### EU-Türkiye Customs Union and the A.TR

The customs union covers industrial products. The European Commission states that goods produced in, or released into free circulation in, the customs union can circulate in its territory with zero duties and zero quotas, as long as they travel with an A.TR movement certificate. The A.TR proves customs status, not origin. You still pay import VAT in your country.

### UK-Turkey trade agreement

The UK and Turkey have had a trade agreement in effect since the end of the Brexit transition period. According to GOV.UK, goods must meet the UK-Turkey preferential rules of origin to get the preferential tariff. If they do not, they can still be imported at the standard tariff. For garments, the product-specific rule can depend on where the fabric was made, so tell us your requirements and let your broker confirm before you order.

### Textile labelling in the EU

EU Regulation 1007/2011 requires a fibre composition label using the regulated fibre names, in the official language or languages of the country where the suit is sold. Suits sold in Belgium may need French, Dutch and German, for example. Send us your label text and we sew it in during production. Under private label, your brand name, size and care labels go in at the same time.

## FAQ

### Do suits from Turkey pay customs duty in the EU?

Suits are industrial goods covered by the EU-Türkiye Customs Union, which applies zero customs duties when the goods travel with an A.TR movement certificate. Import VAT still applies. Ask your customs broker to confirm for your commodity code.

### What about duty for UK buyers?

The UK-Turkey trade agreement gives preferential tariffs to goods that meet its rules of origin. Goods that do not qualify pay the standard UK tariff. Your broker should check this before the first shipment.

### Can you sew our labels in the local language?

Yes. Send us your fibre composition, care and brand label text in the languages you need, and we add them during production.

### What is the minimum order for European retailers?

One lot from stock, for example 6 suits of one model in a size run. Private label starts at 100 units per style.

### How do you ship to Europe?

Mostly by truck, as full loads or groupage. Air for urgent orders and samples, sea for some large orders. We quote per destination.
