## Three ways to work with us

You can buy from stock, order production under your brand, or have us produce your own design. Each has a different minimum and timeline.

### Wholesale from stock

Many catalog models are ready in Istanbul and sold in size-run lots, one suit per size. A lot is usually 6 or 7 suits. It is the quickest way to start: no sampling, no production wait, and you can mix as many models as you like. Stock suits carry our standard labels. [Wholesale suits from stock](/wholesale-suits)

### Private label

We produce our models, or your adjusted version of them, under your brand. You choose fabric, colour, fit, lining, buttons, labels, hangtags and garment bags. The minimum is 100 units per style. Most retailers and online menswear brands that want their own line start here. [Private label suits](/private-label-suits)

### OEM manufacturing

You send a tech pack or sample garment, and we produce it to your specifications: pattern, grading, fit approval and bulk production. We make your design for you and don't sell it to other buyers. The minimum is 100 units per style. [OEM suit manufacturing](/oem-suit-manufacturing)

## How a suit is made in our factory

Every suit goes through the same production steps, whether it is for our stock or for your brand.

1. **Pattern making.** Patterns are drafted or digitized in CAD by our in-house pattern team. New models are checked with 3D prototyping, then sampled and graded across the size range.
2. **Cutting.** Fabric is checked and cut to the graded patterns for each size in the order.
3. **Sewing.** Jackets, trousers and waistcoats are sewn in our own production. Each order is tracked in our ERP system from cutting to packing.
4. **Pressing.** Seams, lapels and finished garments are pressed so the suit holds its shape on the hanger.
5. **Quality control.** Suits are checked during sewing and again at the end: measurements, seams, lining, buttons, pressing and labels.
6. **Packing.** Each suit goes into a garment bag with its hangtag, then into cartons marked with model, sizes and order reference.

## Why make suits in Turkey

Turkey has a long tailoring tradition and a complete supply chain for suits: fabric mills, trims, lining and packaging suppliers are all close to Istanbul. That keeps production runs short and makes small and mid-size orders practical. Istanbul is also within a short flight of Europe, the Middle East, North Africa and Central Asia, so buyers can visit the factory and goods can travel by road as well as air and sea.

Buyers who come to Turkey from Far East suppliers usually do it for shorter lead times, lower minimums and easier communication. Our [guide to sourcing suits from Turkey](/guides/how-to-source-suits-from-turkey) covers costs, quality checks and the questions to ask a supplier.
