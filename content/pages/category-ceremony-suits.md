## What we produce

Ceremony suits are our statement pieces: tuxedos and dinner jackets designed to stand out at galas, weddings, receptions and on stage. As a ceremony suits manufacturer, we make them with the extra handwork these styles need, from sequin fabrics to crystal and beaded details.

They are sold mostly as sets with jacket, trousers and bowtie.

## Styles

- **Sequin tuxedos** in silver, copper, champagne gold and black
- **Crystal and rhinestone tuxedos** with crystal-beaded jackets, sleeves or lapels, including ombre effects
- **Beaded and studded styles**, such as gold beading on sleeves or gold studs on satin lapels
- **Embroidered tuxedos** with floral or line embroidery on black
- **Velvet tuxedos** with satin shawl or wide peak lapels
- **Embellished dinner jackets** in ivory and white

Most of these are shawl lapel with a satin finish, double-breasted and slim fit. Some models use peak lapels or single-button closures.

## Fabrics and fits

| Style | Fabric | Notes |
|---|---|---|

All ceremony suits are fully lined in 100% viscose. Because of the handwork, embellished and sequin styles are priced above our plain tuxedos, and made-to-order lead times depend more on the detail involved. See [fabrics](/fabrics) for more.

## Sizes

Stock ceremony suits come in lots of 7, one per size from EU 46 to 58 (UK and US 36 to 48). Some crystal models run 48 to 58. See the [size guide](/size-guide).
