## What we produce

A Mark Morente three-piece suit is a jacket, trousers and a waistcoat cut from the same fabric. As a 3-piece suits manufacturer in Istanbul, we make them for weddings, formal events and business wear, where the waistcoat gives the outfit a more finished look than a two-piece suit.

The waistcoat is cut to the same fit as the jacket, so the suit looks finished with the jacket on or off. That matters for wedding customers, who often take the jacket off during the evening.

## Styles, fabrics and fits

Our current stock three-piece suits are single-breasted with peak lapels in a slim fit, made in wool blend with a 100% viscose lining. Colours in stock include navy blue, charcoal grey, black, emerald green and brown. For made-to-order runs we can change almost every detail.

| Labels | Our standard labels | Your woven labels, hangtags and garment bags |

Darker colours such as navy, charcoal and black sell all year for business and formal wear. Green, brown and lighter shades sell well as wedding suits and in spring and summer. See [fabrics](/fabrics) for how to choose by climate and price point.

## Sizes

Stock three-piece suits come in EU 46 to 56, which is UK and US 36 to 46. For made-to-order runs you choose the size range and the quantity per size, including larger sizes for [big and tall](/mens-suit-manufacturing/big-tall-suits) customers. See the [size guide](/size-guide) for conversions.

## Lots and minimum order

There are two ways to buy three-piece suits from us:

- **Wholesale from stock:** one lot per model, one suit in each size from EU 46 to 56, so 6 suits per lot. You can mix models and colours. [How wholesale lots work](/wholesale-suits)
- **Made to order:** 100 units per style, in your fabric, colour and fit, with your branding. Production typically takes 12-15 days after sample approval, depending on quantity and fabric. [Private label three-piece suits](/private-label-suits)

Many buyers start with a few stock lots in different colours, see which sell, then order their best colour as a private label run.

## FAQ

### What is included in a three-piece suit?

A jacket, trousers and a matching waistcoat, all in the same fabric.

### What is the minimum order for three-piece suits?

One lot of 6 suits per model from stock (EU 46-56), or 100 units per style for made-to-order production under your label.

Yes, in made-to-order production. A contrast waistcoat is a common choice for wedding collections.

Yes, for made-to-order runs. Send us a reference photo and we will quote it.

### Are your three-piece suits made in Turkey?

Yes. All our three-piece suits are made in our production in Istanbul.
