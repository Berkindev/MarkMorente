## How to reach us

Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram for quotes, catalogs, samples and order questions. We reply within 24 hours on business days.

| Channel | Details | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp | +90 532 171 14 94 | Quotes, catalogs, orders |
| Telegram | @markmorentesuits | Quotes and sample requests |
| Instagram | @mark.morente | New models and factory updates |

We are based in Istanbul, Turkey.

## What to include in your first message

A complete first message gets you a complete quote. Please include:

- Your company name and website or social media page
- Your country and city
- What kind of business you run: wholesale, retail shop, online store, rental, uniforms
- Whether you want stock lots, private label or OEM
- Model codes from our [collections](/collections), or reference photos
- Quantities: number of lots, or units per style and size
- Destination, and whether you have your own forwarder

If you only have some of this, send what you have. We will ask for the rest.

## We work with businesses only

We sell to wholesalers, importers, retailers, wedding and formalwear shops, brands and corporate buyers. We don't sell single suits or make one-off tailored pieces. The smallest order from stock is one lot, usually 6 or 7 suits of one model. Private label and OEM orders start at 100 units per style.
