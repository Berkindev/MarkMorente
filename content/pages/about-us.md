## Our story

Mark Morente was founded in Istanbul and has been producing men's suits for over 20 years. Istanbul has long been the centre of Turkish textiles and tailoring, and our production sits close to the fabric mills, trim suppliers and skilled tailors the city is known for.

Our aim has stayed the same: combine Turkish textile know-how with modern production, so that our buyers get well-made suits at a fair price, in the quantities they need and on time. Today we make our own collections for wholesale and produce private label and OEM suits for other brands.

## What we make

We produce men's two-piece and three-piece suits, double-breasted suits, wedding suits, safari and overshirt suits, linen summer suits and big and tall suits, plus tuxedos and dinner jackets made to order. Fits are slim, modern and classic. Our fabrics range from everyday wool blends to pinstripe suiting and jacquard for occasion wear. See [what we manufacture](/mens-suit-manufacturing) and the current [collections](/collections).

## Capacity

Our daily capacity is more than 1,000 suits. That lets us keep a wide range of models in stock for wholesale buyers while running production orders for private label and OEM customers at the same time. A first order of 100 units per style and a multi-style seasonal programme can both be planned without long waiting times.

## Technology

We invest in the parts of production that decide fit and consistency:

- **CAD pattern design:** patterns are drafted, adjusted and graded digitally, so every size of a model stays in proportion.
- **3D prototyping:** new models are checked digitally before we cut the first sample, which saves sampling rounds.
- **ERP-controlled production:** every order is tracked from cutting to packing, so we always know where your order stands.
- **In-house pattern making and sampling:** our own team makes patterns and samples, which keeps development fast and under our control.

## How we work

Our team covers the whole path from design to shipment: pattern makers, sampling, cutting, sewing, pressing, quality control, packing and export. You talk to us directly on WhatsApp or Telegram, from the first message to delivery.

Quality is checked at every step of production, not only at the end. Each suit is checked for measurements, seams, lining, pressing, buttons and labels before it is packed. For production orders, we approve the sample with you first and produce to that standard.

We reply to enquiries within 24 hours on business days, and we quote every order individually, with price and lead time per model and quantity.

We ship to buyers across [Europe](/markets/europe), [Africa](/markets/africa), the [Middle East](/markets/middle-east), [Central Asia and the Caucasus](/markets/central-asia) and [North America](/markets/north-america).
