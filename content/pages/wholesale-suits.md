## How wholesale lots work

A lot is one model in a full size run, usually one suit in each size. It is the smallest quantity we sell, and the way all our stock is packed and priced.

A typical lot looks like this:

| EU size | 46 | 48 | 50 | 52 | 54 | 56 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suits | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6 |

Lots vary by model. Some models are packed in lots of 7, covering EU 46 to 58. The exact size run of each model is shown on its page in our [collections](/collections). If you need help converting sizes for your market, see the [size guide](/size-guide).

Buying in lots keeps the size curve balanced and means every model reaches you ready to sell across a normal range of customers.

## Why buy from stock

Stock lots are the fastest and lowest-risk way to start with us. There is no sampling, no MOQ of 100 units, and no production wait. You see the actual model in the catalog, choose the lots you want, and we confirm availability.

Stock lots suit buyers who:

- Want to test a new supplier or a new market with a small order
- Need occasion wear quickly, for example before wedding season
- Run a shop or chain that needs many different models in small quantities
- Supply other retailers and want to offer variety

Stock lots carry our standard labels. If you want your own brand on the suits, that is a [private label order](/private-label-suits), which starts at 100 units per style.

## Mixing models in one order

You can mix as many models as you like in one order, as long as each model is ordered in full lots. For example, one order could include two lots of a navy three-piece suit, one lot of a burgundy pinstripe double-breasted suit and one lot of a cream suit with a brown waistcoat.

Mixing is common with wedding and formalwear shops, who usually want a spread of colours and styles rather than depth in one model. Distributors often take several lots of their best-selling models and one lot each of new ones to test.

## In stock and out of stock labels

Every model in our catalog is marked **In Stock** or **Out of Stock**.

- **In Stock** means we have lots of this model ready in Istanbul. We still confirm the exact number of lots when you send your order, because stock moves daily.
- **Out of Stock** means there are no ready lots at the moment. The model is not discontinued. We can tell you when it will be restocked, or produce it for you as a made-to-order run.

If a model you want is out of stock, ask us. Sometimes a restock is already in production.

## Restocking and reorders

We restock our popular models regularly. If a model sells well for you, tell us how many lots you will need and when. We can plan it into production so it is ready when you reorder.

For larger reorders of one model, ask about a production run. Once you are ordering 100 units or more of one style, a made-to-order run often makes more sense, and you can add your own labels at that point.

## What wholesale prices depend on

We don't publish prices because they depend on the order. The main factors are:

- **Model and construction:** a three-piece or double-breasted suit costs more than a plain two-piece suit
- **Fabric:** wool blends, jacquards, velvet and linen blends are priced differently
- **Number of lots:** larger orders are quoted accordingly
- **Packing and shipping:** garment bags, cartons and the destination

Send us the model codes and number of lots, and we will quote each model with availability.

## Wholesale buyers only

We sell stock lots to businesses: retailers, wholesalers, importers, wedding and formalwear shops, rental companies and online menswear stores. We don't split lots or sell single suits. If you are buying for yourself, a local retailer that stocks Turkish suits is the better option.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum wholesale order?

The minimum is one lot of one model. Most lots contain 6 or 7 suits, one in each size of the run.

### Can I choose the sizes in a lot?

Stock lots are packed in a fixed size run, so the sizes are set. If you need a different size curve, a made-to-order run of 100 units per style lets you choose the breakdown.

### Can I mix different models in one order?

Yes. You can combine any number of models, as long as each one is ordered in full lots.

### Do stock suits come with my brand label?

No, stock lots carry our standard labels. Private label production with your branding starts at 100 units per style.

### What happens if a model is out of stock?

Ask us. We will tell you whether a restock is planned, or quote it as a made-to-order run.
