## What we produce

The two-piece suit is the core of most menswear ranges, and the model we make in the widest variety. As a 2-piece suits manufacturer, we produce jacket-and-trouser suits for business wear, weddings, summer events and everyday retail.

Our range includes:

- **Single-breasted business suits** with notch or peak lapels, for office and formal wear
- **Double-breasted suits** with peak lapels, including models with gold metal buttons
- **Linen-blend summer suits** with patch pockets, for warm climates and resort weddings
- **Fashion models** such as asymmetric fronts and contemporary lapels for younger customers

## Sizes

Stock two-piece suits are sold in EU 46 to 56, and some models run to 58. In UK and US sizing that is 36 to 46 or 48. For made-to-order production you choose the range and the quantity per size. Matching trouser sizes follow the usual drop for each fit. See the [size guide](/size-guide).

## Lots and minimum order

- **Wholesale from stock:** one lot per model, one suit per size, usually 6 suits. You can mix models and colours in one order. [Wholesale suits](/wholesale-suits)
- **Made to order:** 100 units per style, with your choice of fabric, colour, fit and branding. Production typically takes 12-15 days after sample approval. [Private label suits](/private-label-suits)

For retail chains running a core business suit programme, a made-to-order run in two or three colours is usually the most cost-effective route. You get consistent fabric and fit across reorders, under your own label.

One lot per model from stock, usually 6 suits, or 100 units per style for made-to-order production.

### Can I buy jackets and trousers separately?

Our suits are sold as sets. If you need separates for a made-to-order programme, ask us and we will quote it.

### Do you make linen suits?

Yes. We stock linen-blend two-piece summer suits and can produce them in other colours from 100 units per style.

### Can you produce business suits for corporate uniforms?

Yes. We produce the same suit across your full size range, with your labels, from 100 units per style.

### Which fits do you offer?

Slim, modern and classic fits. Most of our stock models are slim fit.
