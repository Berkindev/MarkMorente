## Suits made in Istanbul, sold to businesses worldwide

Mark Morente is a factory, not a trading office. We have been making men's suits in Istanbul for more than 20 years, and everything we sell is cut and sewn in our own production.

- **Ready stock in lots:** many catalog models are in stock and ship as size-run lots, so you can start with one lot per model.
- **Your brand, our factory:** made-to-order production from 100 units per style, with your fabric, fit, labels and packaging.

## Two ways to buy

You can buy from our stock in lots, or order a production run made for your brand. Most new buyers start with stock lots to test their market, then move to made-to-order once they know what sells.

| | Stock lots | Made to order |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | Our catalog models, ready to ship | Your models or our models, customized |
| Minimum | One lot per model (usually 6 or 7 suits) | 100 units per style |
| Branding | Our standard labels | Your woven labels, hangtags, garment bags |
| Fabric and colour | As shown in the catalog | Your choice |
| Timing | Ships once stock is confirmed and paid | Production typically 12-15 days |

Read more about [buying wholesale from stock](/wholesale-suits) or [private label production](/private-label-suits). If you already have a tech pack, see [OEM manufacturing](/oem-suit-manufacturing).

## What we manufacture

We produce the full range of men's tailoring for retail and occasion wear: business suits, three-piece suits with waistcoat, tuxedos and dinner jackets, wedding and ceremony suits, and big and tall sizes. Fits are slim, modern and classic. Browse the categories below or see the current models in our [collections](/collections).

## Built for wholesale buyers

We work with businesses that resell suits: wholesalers, importers, distributors, menswear retailers, boutique chains, department stores, online menswear brands, wedding and formalwear shops, rental companies and corporate uniform buyers.

We don't sell single suits or make one-off tailored pieces. If you are looking for one suit for your own wedding, we are not the right supplier. The smallest order we accept is one lot.

## How ordering works

Ordering takes four steps, all handled directly with us on WhatsApp or Telegram.

1. **Send an enquiry** on WhatsApp or Telegram with your company name, country, the models you like and rough quantities.
2. **Get a quote** with price per model, quantity, lead time and shipping options. Samples are available when needed.
3. **Confirm the order.** Payment terms are agreed per order, then stock is reserved or production is scheduled.
4. **We check, pack and ship.** Every order goes through quality control before it is packed and handed to the carrier or your forwarder.

The full process, with what to send in your first message, is on [how to order](/how-to-order).

## Why manufacture in Turkey

Turkey is one of the closest full-package suit producers to Europe, Africa, the Middle East and Central Asia. Fabric, trims and finishing are available locally, so production runs are short and buyers can visit the factory easily. ## Quality and certifications

Our production is ISO 9001 certified and we work with OEKO-TEX Standard 100 fabrics. Patterns are made in CAD, production is tracked in an ERP system, and suits are checked at each stage of sewing and again before packing. Daily capacity is more than 1,000 suits, which means a 100-unit style or a container order can be planned without long queues.
