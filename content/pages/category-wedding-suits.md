## What we produce

A wedding range needs three things: a standout look for the groom, matching suits for groomsmen, and smart options for guests. As a wedding suit manufacturer, we make all three, so a wedding shop can buy its whole range from one supplier.

- **Groom:** tuxedos, dinner jackets, three-piece suits and embellished styles that stand out in photos
- **Groomsmen:** matching two-piece or three-piece suits, ordered in the same model and colour across sizes
- **Guests:** business-style suits, linen summer suits and double-breasted suits that work beyond the wedding day

## Sizes

Stock wedding suits come in EU 46 to 56, and most tuxedos run 46 to 58. In UK and US sizes that is 36 to 46 or 48. Groomsmen orders usually need a wide range of sizes in one model, which is exactly how our lots are built. See the [size guide](/size-guide).

## Lots and minimum order

- **From stock:** one lot per model, one suit per size, 6 or 7 suits. A wedding shop can take one lot each of several groom styles and two or three lots of a groomsmen model. [Wholesale suits](/wholesale-suits)
- **Private label wedding suits:** 100 units per style, with your fabric, colours and branding. Production typically takes 12-15 days after sample approval, so plan collections ahead of the wedding season. [Private label](/private-label-suits)

We make wedding suits for businesses only. If you are a groom looking for your own suit, we are not the right supplier, as the smallest order is one lot.

## FAQ

### Do you sell wedding suits to grooms directly?

No. We sell to businesses only. The smallest order is one lot of one model.

### What is the minimum order for wedding suits?

One lot per model from stock, 6 or 7 suits. Custom wedding suits for B2B buyers under your label start at 100 units per style.

### Can I get matching suits for groomsmen?

Yes. Order several lots of the same model, or a made-to-order run in your chosen size breakdown.

### When should a wedding shop place a private label order?

Allow for sampling, 12-15 days of production and shipping. Contact us well before your peak wedding season so the goods arrive in time.

### Do you make Turkish wedding suits in bright colours?

Yes. Our catalog includes green, burgundy, red, bronze and other colours, and we can produce your own palette from 100 units per style.
