## What we produce

We make two-piece suits, three-piece suits and tuxedos in extended sizes for retailers who serve big and tall customers. As a big and tall suits manufacturer, we adjust the pattern for larger sizes, not only the measurements. A plus size suit needs more room in the chest, waist and seat, and a different balance in the jacket, or it will pull and crease.

## How we fit larger sizes

Simply grading a slim pattern up to a 4XL or 6XL rarely gives a good fit. For big and tall made-to-order runs we:

- Grade the pattern with extra allowance through the waist and seat for bigger sizes
- Adjust jacket length and sleeve length for taller customers
- Check the largest sizes as samples before bulk production
- Use classic or modern fits, which give more ease than slim fit

Our pattern work is done in CAD by our in-house team, so these changes are planned per size rather than applied as a single rule.

## FAQ

### What is the largest size you produce?

Stock models go up to EU 58. Made-to-order runs can go up to 6XL, around EU 62, depending on the model.

### Do you just scale up standard patterns?

No. For extended sizes we adjust the pattern for a fuller chest, waist and seat and check the largest sizes as samples.

### What is the minimum order for big and tall suits?

One lot per model from stock, or 100 units per style for made-to-order production with your own size breakdown.

### Can you make long lengths for tall customers?

For made-to-order runs we can adjust jacket and sleeve length. Tell us the lengths you need and we will confirm them for the model.

### Can big and tall suits carry my label?

Yes. Private label orders from 100 units per style include your labels, hangtags and garment bags.
